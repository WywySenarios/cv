# Tailor the summary line

Given the selected atoms and the posting, pick the best existing summary
variant, or draft one new line of at most two sentences.

## Rules

- Ground every claim in the selected atoms. Never assert a skill, tool, or
  experience the atoms do not contain.
- Mirror the posting's vocabulary where it matches the atoms.
- Keep it to the space of one or two lines.
